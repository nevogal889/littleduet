## Name-collision check (done 2026-10-16)

**Result: I found no app called exactly "Little Duet" on the App Store or Google Play web listings. The risk is medium, because similar names already exist in the same space. A trademark search is still open.**

What I searched:
- Web searches for `"Little Duet" app piano`, `site:apps.apple.com "Little Duet"`, `site:play.google.com "Little Duet"`, `"Little Duet" Google Play OR App Store`, and `"Little Duet" trademark OR brand OR company kids music`. None returned a store listing named Little Duet.
- I tried to open the Google Play web search page (`play.google.com/store/search?q="little duet"&c=apps`), but the request timed out waiting for permission. **Search the Play Store app for "Little Duet" by hand before you reserve the name.**

Nearby names to know about:
- **Duet Friends: Pet Music Games**, by Amanotes, on the App Store (Music category, rated 4+, contains ads). Same word, music category, cartoon style that appeals to kids. This is the closest risk for search confusion and trademark conflict. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/duet-friends-pet-music-games/id6450445617
- **Little Piano - Learn to Play**, on the App Store. Same "Little" + piano pattern in the kids space. https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id757811606
- **Little Melodies**, on the App Store (found in search; I could not open the listing). https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6747955142
- **Halilit "Duet"**, a toddler percussion shaker. This is a physical kids' musical instrument, not an app, so a trademark in the toy or instrument class could matter. https://modernbrands.com.au/products/halilit-duet-2
- **"Little Duets" Op. 38 by Mazas**: public-domain sheet music. This is generic usage and shows "little duet" is a descriptive phrase in music, which weakens exclusivity for anyone, including us.
- **Duet** (2013 puzzle game) and other "Duet" games: different category.

What to do (also listed in release-checklist.md, open legal items):
1. Reserve the name in App Store Connect early (step 6 of the checklist). Apple rejects a name that's already in use, so this is the fastest real test.
2. Search the USPTO (Trademark Search), EUIPO (eSearch) and WIPO Global Brand Database for LITTLE DUET and DUET in classes 9 (software), 41 (education and entertainment) and 15 (musical instruments).
3. If anything blocks it, switch to Alt B, "Bench for Two: Kids Piano", and run the same checks.
