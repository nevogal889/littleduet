## Core loop

Pick the next mission on the path, watch a short tutorial, then play exercises and songs on a real piano while the app listens through the microphone (or MIDI) and scores each note for pitch and timing, earning 1–3 stars per part.

## Teaching & video mechanism

### 1. Lesson format: what a lesson is made of

- **Unit = mission.** "Missions are... a group of songs, video tutorials, and trainers with its own theme"; you can skip to any mission, but "you need to play each task within a mission to complete it." ([4](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-syllabus-works))
- **Video then play.** A short intro video explains what you are about to practice, then you play the exercise ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)). TapSmart describes each lesson as "a video, exercises, and workouts where you play along with a track" ([27](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/piano-yousician-virtual-piano-tutor-designed-teachers/)). The first lesson has the teacher introduce finding middle C, then you play middle C in your first song ([1](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204738372-Get-started-with-Yousician-piano)).
- **The play screen.** Piano has four notation styles ([6](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208678605-Notation-options-in-piano)):
  - *Enhanced*: sheet with finger colours and the note name on every note; bar length shows how long to hold.
  - *Falling notes*: blue for left hand, purple for right hand, finger numbers 1–5, note names, bar length = duration.
  - *Colored sheet*: real note shapes with finger colours.
  - *Standard sheet*: plain black and white.
  Press reviews describe a side-scrolling staff with note names and a compressed on-screen keyboard that lights the next key, with a moving line showing when to play ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/), [28](https://chiarunpiano.co.uk/blog/2019/can-you-learn-to-play-the-piano-using-an-app-part-1)). TopTenReviews says proper notation "only opens up at 'level three'" ([26](https://toptenreviews.com/yousician-for-piano-review)).
- **Backing track.** Exercises play along with a backing track and you play against it (Wikipedia: users "play along to a backing track", [17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician)).
- **Length.** Not verified. No source gave video lengths or a minutes-per-lesson figure. **Inference (low):** the intro videos are short (under 2 minutes), because reviewers describe them as a quick explanation before each exercise.

### 2. Input and feedback

**Input paths** ([2](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204799551-Choosing-your-set-up-for-piano), [3](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/203751452-Game-Settings-tab)):
- **Microphone (default).** Put the device near the piano and keep app volume around half, because louder playback interferes with recognition. Headphones help.
- **USB-B, MIDI In/Out through an interface, or Bluetooth MIDI.** Bluetooth must be paired again every session, and Yousician warns that "wireless technology can be too slow" and adds latency.
- **Built-in on-screen keyboard** on touch devices, "only available for beginner exercises" ([3](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/203751452-Game-Settings-tab), [7](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/46277319890449-Piano-sound-recognition-issues)).

**Calibration and latency.**
- The first Practice or Play opens a calibration screen. You play the keys shown, and "the key shows a check mark ✔︎, when Yousician hears you play it" ([8](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005198305-Testing-piano-input)).
- The advanced audio settings hold the rest ([3](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/203751452-Game-Settings-tab)):
  - an "Input activity" indicator (dots light up when it hears you)
  - Buffer size (smaller means less latency but worse performance)
  - Latency calibration
  - audio driver choice (AAudio on Android adjusts the buffer automatically, [7](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/46277319890449-Piano-sound-recognition-issues))
  - MIDI sensitivity
- TopTenReviews still noticed latency on Apple devices ([26](https://toptenreviews.com/yousician-for-piano-review)).

**Recognition limits Yousician documents itself** ([7](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/46277319890449-Piano-sound-recognition-issues)): background noise, app volume too loud, keyboard effects (turn them off), and a piano "tuned to a different octave" may not be recognized. Independent tests found that it works best next to an acoustic upright, that it missed F major chords, and that MIDI keyboards needed high volume ([27](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/piano-yousician-virtual-piano-tutor-designed-teachers/)). American Songwriter found accuracy "pretty darn good" and better on MIDI ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)).

**How the recognition works, from the patents** (Yousician Oy, Helsinki; research lead Anssi Klapuri, [18](https://patents.justia.com/assignee/yousician-oy)):
- **US 2014/0041511 / US 9,218,748** (filed 2012 as Ovelin), [20](https://patents.justia.com/patent/20140041511):
  - Audio from a mic or line input, or MIDI, is turned into frequency components with amplitudes ("pitch and salience").
  - It recognizes several simultaneous notes, citing Klapuri's 2006 summing-harmonic-amplitudes method.
  - The expected notes and chords of an exercise are converted into expected frequencies. The system sums the weighted amplitudes of the correct components and scales that into a per-note point score.
  - Latency compensation runs before evaluation.
  - Instant feedback can be visuals, sounds, a screen flash, or changes to the backing track. "The exercise can also slow or pause until the correct note is played."
- **US 10,198,960 / 10,235,898** (2017), [21](https://patents.justia.com/patent/10198960):
  - Short segments of the user's audio are compared in real time with the harmonic parameters of the track.
  - Performances from many reference users are pooled by median, histogram and weighted voting. Where references agree, a part is treated as "fixed" and must be matched. Where they vary, it is "dynamic" and gets more tolerance.
  - The pooled data updates the feedback settings.
- **US 12,046,221** (score follows the player using activity, tonality and tempo) and **US 12,347,408 / EP 4 270 373** (identify the song being played from live audio plus play history), [18](https://patents.justia.com/assignee/yousician-oy), [22](https://patentsgazette.uspto.gov/week26/OG/html/1536-1/US12347408-20250701.html), [23](https://data.epo.org/publication-server/rest/v1.2/patents/EP4270373NWA1/document.html). This is their newer direction: a score that follows the player, not a player who follows a fixed scroll.
- **Inference (medium):** the shipped piano recognizer descends from the 2012 multi-pitch method, now with ML on top. The patents do not say which method the shipping app uses. A 2017 job post title "Music transcription algorithm developer (Anssi Klapuri)" ([auditory.org](https://auditory.org/postings/2017/382.html)) shows an in-house transcription team, but the post body was not readable.
- Search results also pointed to the ISMIR 2006 paper PDF (https://archives.ismir.net/ismir2006/paper/000125.pdf) and an open-source implementation of Klapuri's multi-pitch method in Essentia (https://essentia.upf.edu/reference/std_MultiPitchKlapuri.html). I did not open either, so the paper link is unverified.

**Wrong notes, waiting and scoring:**
- **Wait mode: not found.** The Practice/Play help article lists tempo, auto-adjust speed, loop, metronome, mute song, loop lead-in, hands toggle and "Play for me" (hear the note on a synth), with no wait mode ([5](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)). **Inference (medium):** Yousician keeps the song moving and adapts speed instead of waiting for the player. The 2012 patent's "pause until correct" option does not appear in today's piano help docs.
- **Auto-adjust speed** is the in-song adaptivity: "The app controls the song speed and adjusts it up or down depending on how you play", from 25 % to 125 % in 5 % steps ([5](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)).
- **Timing feedback.** Notes are judged early or late as well as right or wrong. A piano teacher found that "even when playing the notes correctly I was still informed of "lateness" or "earliness"" ([28](https://chiarunpiano.co.uk/blog/2019/can-you-learn-to-play-the-piano-using-an-app-part-1)).
- **Score.**
  - Each song part earns 1–3 stars, silver to gold, and gold needs accuracy and good timing ([10](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208014795-How-song-score-is-calculated)).
  - In Play mode a points multiplier starts at 3x and rises to 5x with correct, well-timed notes ([5](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)).
  - "Your best" is the sum of your best result on each part across all plays ([10](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208014795-How-song-score-is-calculated)).
- **Two modes.**
  - *Practice* loops the song at the end, lets you change tempo and practice one hand at a time, and does not advance the path.
  - *Play* is the scored run that "progress[es] in the learning path" ([5](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)).

### 3. Curriculum and pacing

- **Themes and levels.** Three themes: Classical (9 levels, solo piano), Pop (9 levels, groove-based band playing) and Knowledge (5 levels, theory through mini games). Missions are grouped by level from Basics upward, and "thousands of exercises" run from basics to level 9 ([4](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-syllabus-works)).
- **No hard gating and no placement test.** "The path is unlocked", you can switch themes any time, and even experienced players are advised to start at the first mission ([4](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-syllabus-works)). Onboarding asks for an experience level ([29](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/yousician-your-personal-music-teacher)). One Trustpilot reviewer could not skip ahead ([reviews.csv](reviews.csv)). This conflicts with the help article: **unverified**, and it may differ by version.
- **Adaptivity, from the patent** US 9,767,705 ([19](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9767705)):
  - The learner has a skill profile (rhythmic, melodic, motoric and other areas) on the same scale as each song's difficulty profile.
  - Few mistakes at level N is evidence of skill ≥ N. Mistakes are attributed per note or chord to a skill area.
  - Difficulty features include the time gap to neighbouring notes, simultaneous note count and note density. The simplest model is the "average inverse of the time gaps".
  - The system picks songs at or slightly above the weakest skill, and can create **reductions** of songs (remove grace notes, simplify chords) to fit a level.
- **Inference (medium):** the shipped app uses this for recommendations ("Daily Session", "Missing Stars", Workouts) more than for reordering the path. The path itself is hand-curated by "our team of educators" ([1](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204738372-Get-started-with-Yousician-piano)).
- **Song arrangements by level.** Not verified in the docs. The patent describes level-specific reductions ([19](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9767705)). **Inference (low):** popular songs appear in several arrangements.

### 4. Guidance: who teaches

- **Real-person video teachers.** Onboarding includes "a video [that] introduces their in-app teacher... a real person whose voice guides the quests and songs" ([29](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/yousician-your-personal-music-teacher)). Marketing says lessons are "created by real music teachers and skilled piano players" ([15](https://yousician.com/piano)). No teacher names are given publicly.
- **Posture and keys.** The tutor demonstrates a relaxed hand shape, but a piano teacher noted "the tutor did not say a word on how high/low or near/far from the piano to sit" and that beginner classes give "no tuition on rhythm or keyboard geography" ([28](https://chiarunpiano.co.uk/blog/2019/can-you-learn-to-play-the-piano-using-an-app-part-1)).
- **Finding keys without reading.** A colour-coded virtual piano shows where each note is and which finger to use ([15](https://yousician.com/piano)). Finger colours and note names sit on every note in the Enhanced and Falling views ([6](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208678605-Notation-options-in-piano)). This is the screen equivalent of light-up keys. The child looks at the screen keyboard, not the real one. The teacher blog argues the flashing keyboard "discourages students from actually thinking about the notes" ([28](https://chiarunpiano.co.uk/blog/2019/can-you-learn-to-play-the-piano-using-an-app-part-1)).

### 5. Kids and parent angle

- **No kids mode or kids curriculum was found.** Common Sense Media rates it **9+** ([29](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/yousician-your-personal-music-teacher)). The Spanish CSM page shows 10+ ([link](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/es/node/68845/user-reviews/adult)).
- **Family = separate accounts, not child profiles.** Premium+ Family covers the manager plus up to 3 members, each with their own free account and individual progress ([12](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/360019797498-FAQ-Family-plan), [16](https://yousician.com/blog/premium-family-memberships)). The price is $209.99 per year per American Songwriter ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)).
- **Parent visibility.** A weekly Activity report email every Monday shows notes played and play time ([4](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-syllabus-works)).
- **Kids paying.** A Trustpilot reviewer wrote that her child bought Premium on two phones ([reviews.csv](reviews.csv)). Parental purchase gating matters.

### 6. Motivation

- Stars: 1–3 per part, silver and gold. A "Missing Stars" list and a Workouts tab let you go back for them ([9](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/201558312-How-to-navigate-in-Yousician), [10](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208014795-How-song-score-is-calculated)).
- Points multiplier 3x→5x ([5](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204796991-Practice-and-Play-modes-in-piano)).
- **Weekly** streak, "how many weeks you've played in a row", not daily ([9](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/201558312-How-to-navigate-in-Yousician)).
- Session goal in stars or minutes, badges, a Daily Session shortcut and Riff of the Day ([9](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/201558312-How-to-navigate-in-Yousician)).
- Weekly Challenges with their own leaderboard, plus song leaderboards (My level / Global / Friends) ([10](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/208014795-How-song-score-is-calculated), [25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)).
- A 30-day challenge with hashtag sharing ([11](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/360017752598-30-day-playing-challenge)).
- XP and leagues: not found.

## Monetization

- **Free tier.** Limited daily use: "one lesson per day" ([17](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yousician)), or "a few minutes a day" ([29](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/app-reviews/yousician-your-personal-music-teacher)).
- **Paid tiers.**
  - *Premium*: unlimited lessons on one instrument.
  - *Premium+*: all 5 instruments plus all songs.
  - *Premium+ Family*: 4 accounts.
  ([13](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005189525-Premium-options-available-in-Yousician))
- **Trial.** 7-day Premium+ trial ([14](https://yousician.com/lp/piano)), with a reminder on day 5 ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)).
- **Prices (US, vary by store and date).**
  - Premium+ $139.99/yr, Premium $89.99/yr, Family $209.99/yr ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)).
  - Piano by Yousician iOS IAPs: Premium $19.99/mo or $119.99/yr; Premium+ $29.99/mo, $59.99/quarter, or $139.99 or $179.99/yr ([30](https://apppricinglab.com/app/apple/1469033443)).
  - UK main app: Premium+ from £13.99/mo to £159.99/yr ([31](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/yousician-learn-play-guitar/id959883039)).
- **Inference (high):** heavy price testing and regional pricing. A Trustpilot reviewer was told region affects the plan.

## Lessons for Little Duet

Ideas only. No copying of Yousician's content, assets, copy or patented methods.

**Worth learning from**
1. **Mission = video + "your turn" tasks, completed as a set.** This maps directly onto Little Duet's 3–5-minute classes: a short teacher segment, then one or two interactive play tasks, and the class counts as done only when every task is done.
2. **Calibration as a playful first step.** Yousician's "play the key shown, see a check mark" screen ([8](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/115005198305-Testing-piano-input)) is the right pattern for the mandatory tutorial class. It doubles as the mic permission check and a "find middle C" moment.
3. **A visible "I hear you" indicator** (input activity dots, [3](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/203751452-Game-Settings-tab)). Parents need to see that the mic is working before they blame the child.
4. **Publish setup rules up front.** Yousician's own list: app volume at about half, effects off, quiet room, headphones ([2](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204799551-Choosing-your-set-up-for-piano), [7](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/46277319890449-Piano-sound-recognition-issues)). For Little Duet the teacher voice and backing audio come out of the same phone that is listening, so duck the teacher audio while listening for the child's note (inference, high).
5. **Weekly, not daily, streaks** ([9](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/201558312-How-to-navigate-in-Yousician)). This is kinder to families with toddlers.
6. **A Monday report email to the parent** ([4](https://support.yousician.com/hc/en-us/articles/204697992-How-the-piano-syllabus-works)). Cheap to build and high value for the paying adult.
7. **Song reductions by level** (the patent idea, [19](https://patents.google.com/patent/US9767705)). The general idea is simplifying the same song (one finger → five-finger → both hands). This is ordinary pedagogy and fits public-domain songs at Little Duet's difficulty levels. Avoid their claimed skill-profile matching method.
8. **Day-5 trial reminder** ([25](https://americansongwriter.com/yousician-piano-review/)). Billing anger dominates their reviews (see below), so be generous and explicit.

**Avoid**
1. **Timing judgment for 2–6-year-olds.** "Late/early" feedback frustrated even an adult teacher ([28](https://chiarunpiano.co.uk/blog/2019/can-you-learn-to-play-the-piano-using-an-app-part-1)). Little Duet should wait for the right note (pitch only) and never score timing.
2. **A moving scroll the child must keep up with.** Yousician adapts speed (25–125 %) rather than waiting. For toddlers, the music should stop and wait.
3. **Chords and polyphony on the mic.** Even Yousician misses chords on mic ([27](https://www.tapsmart.com/apps/piano-yousician-virtual-piano-tutor-designed-teachers/)). Little Duet's single-note scope is the right call.
4. **Leaderboards, multipliers and global ranking.** These are wrong for preschoolers.
5. **Billing friction.** Of 20 Trustpilot reviews, 8 are about charges after cancelling, auto-renewal or wrong amounts ([32](https://no.trustpilot.com/review/yousician.com)). Add a parental gate on purchases (a child bought Premium on two phones).
6. **Screen-keyboard dependence.** Teacher critique ([28](https://chiarunpiano.co.uk/blog/2019/can-you-learn-to-play-the-piano-using-an-app-part-1)): if the child stares at the phone keyboard, they don't learn the real one. Have the video teacher point at the real keys and fade on-screen hints over a class.
