# Little Duet: Market Research for Ages 2–6 Music and Early Learning

**Research date:** 8 November 2026
**Question:** Who competes for 2–6-year-olds' music time and parents' money in 2026, and where is the open space for a pioneer?
**Scope:** This doc leaves out the adult and teen piano apps we already covered (Simply Piano, Flowkey, Skoove, Yousician, Playground Sessions, Hoffman Academy, Piano Maestro, Lumi/The ONE). They appear only where there is news specific to kids.

**How to read the numbers**
- Every figure links to the page it came from. Store-listing prices, ratings and counts reflect what the page showed when I fetched it.
- Figures from third-party trackers (AppGoblin, apppricinglab, Subger, market-research firms) are **estimates**, and they are labelled that way.
- AppGoblin's monthly-revenue estimates were clearly unreliable. It showed "<$10K" for Sago Mini World, which has 6.2M installs, so I left them out.

**What the table shows**
- **No product combines all four** of these: (a) real-instrument instruction for ages 2–6, (b) a parent as an active co-player, (c) listening feedback, and (d) app-native, short sessions.
- **Music Together and Kindermusik** have the parent role but no instrument skill or feedback, and they cost about $99/mo for online classes.
- **Prodigies** comes closest. It offers short videos, physical bells and parent guidance, but no listening and no duet mechanic, and its reach is modest at "10,000+ families".
- **The scaled players** (Lingokids, Khan Kids, Sago, CoComelon, Pinkfong) win on reach. They treat music as passive or tap-to-play content for one child alone.

## 3. AI in kids' learning, 2025–2026

**What launched**
- **Voice-listening tutors work when they are narrow.** Ello's reading coach listens to kids reading aloud, costs $14.99/mo and holds a 4.8 rating on 2.9K ratings. [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/read-with-ello/id1536720182)
- **Miko:** revenue reached ₹358 crore in FY24, and Entrackr reported the company raising $155M at about a $550M valuation. [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/exclusive/exclusive-robotic-maker-miko-to-raise-155-mn-at-550-mn-valuation-9731064)
- **Music-specific AI for under-6s is almost nonexistent.** A 2026 survey of kids' AI music apps names only teacher-workflow tools and a 4+ hybrid programme. [Furtados, Aug 2026](https://furtadosschoolofmusic.com/blog/how-ai-practice-apps-are-changing-the-way-kids-learn-music-in-2026/)

**The backlash (the big 2025–26 story)**
- **PIRG** reported that FoloToy's Kumma bear and Alilo's bunny, both running on ChatGPT-class models, gave sexual content and directions to knives and matches. OpenAI suspended the developer (Dec 2025). [FOX19/InvestigateTV](https://www.fox19.com/2025/12/12/consumer-safety-report-warns-disturbing-responses-ai-powered-toys/)
- **Mattel and OpenAI** pushed their first product past holiday 2025 and refocused on "families and older customers". Senators Blackburn and Blumenthal wrote to toy makers. [PIRG](https://pirg.org/media-center/statement-mattel-openai-delay-ai-product-launch-as-senators-demand-transparency-from-toy-companies/); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/12/15/mattel-openai-toys-kids)
- **Common Sense Media** tested Grem, Bondu and Miko 3 and found 27% of responses inappropriate. Its advice: **no AI toys for children under 5**, and "extreme caution" for ages 6–12. [CapRadio, Feb 2026](https://www.capradio.org/articles/2026/02/02/study-warns-ai-toys-pose-unacceptable-risks-to-young-children/)
- **Bondu** exposed "tens of thousands" of children's chat logs through a public portal, and Sen. Hassan demanded answers (Feb 2026). [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/03/ai-toy-bondu-chat-data-exposure-hassan)
- **Senators Duckworth and Gillibrand** asked the FTC (12 Mar 2026) to act on deceptive educational claims, require marketing that "AI can't replace human teaching", and enforce COPPA. [Letter PDF](https://www.gillibrand.senate.gov/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/260312aitoyslettertoftc1.pdf)

**Regulation**
- The amended FTC COPPA Rule took effect 23 Jun 2025, with **full compliance required by 22 Apr 2026**. It requires:
  - separate consent before sharing children's data with third parties
  - a written security programme
  - a published data-retention policy that bans indefinite retention

  [NatLawReview, Aug 2025](https://natlawreview.com/article/amendments-ftc-coppa-rule-now-effect)

**What parents want and fear** (Common Sense parent survey, n=1,004, Dec 2025)
- 49% have bought or considered an AI toy.
- Only 19% want the toy to be a companion.
- More than 8 in 10 are worried about data collection.
- Nearly 3 in 4 fear the toy will say something inappropriate.
- Parents hope for learning and good behaviour.

[Common Sense, Jan 2026](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/research/ai-in-the-toy-box-how-parents-view-ai-enabled-toys-for-young-children); [CapRadio](https://www.capradio.org/articles/2026/02/02/study-warns-ai-toys-pose-unacceptable-risks-to-young-children/)

**Implication:** parents want AI that **teaches**, not AI that **befriends**. Little Duet's scripted teacher voice with on-device listening fits that demand, and can say so in its marketing.

## 5. Grandparent and remote-family engagement

**Software that worked**
- **Caribu** (video calls with shared books and games): launched 2017, about 60K registered users at the time of the Encore profile, then named one of Apple's best apps of 2020. Mattel acquired it in Nov 2022. It still runs as a family-shared subscription at £7.99/mo or £79.99/yr. [Encore](https://encore.org/max-tuchman/); [Built In](https://builtin.com/articles/mattel-acquires-caribu); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/caribu/id763451959)
  - Grandparents were its most dedicated users. Almost 50% of surveyed users said they would be "devastated" if it went away. [Encore](https://encore.org/max-tuchman/)

**Hardware that failed, and apps that stalled**
- **Meta Portal Story Time** (2019–2020): AR stories turned the adult reader into a character, and usage was popular in the pandemic. [Nexus Studios](https://nexusstudios.com/work/portal-storytime-ar/)
  - Meta ended consumer Portal in 2022. IDC estimated about 600K units in 2020 and 800K in 2021, about 1% of the smart-display market. [TechSpot](https://www.techspot.com/news/94898-meta-killing-off-portal-video-calling-devices-consumers.html)
- **Amazon Glow** ($300 kids' video-call projector) was discontinued in Oct 2022, about a year after its launch. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/10/04/amazon-axes-glow-video-calling-device-for-kids.html)
- **Readeo** (shared e-books plus video): $9.99/mo or $99.99/yr, Gold Family $14.99/mo. It had 49 ratings and was last updated in Nov 2023, so it looks dormant. [AppAdvice](https://appadvice.com/app/readeo-virtual-storytime/1546681587); [Cool Mom Tech, 2021](https://coolmomtech.com/2021/01/readeo-ebook-app/)

**Behaviour data**
- AARP surveyed 1,157 grandparents of children under 6 in 2020; the sample is nonprobability. [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/research/topics/technology/info-2021/video-chat-grandparents-grandchildren-pandemic.html)
  - About 86% video chat with their grandchildren.
  - The most common call length is 5–15 minutes.
  - Activities include **reading, singing and peek-a-boo**.
  - Parents on the child's side help by directing attention (47%) and asking questions (41%).
- AARP's 2026 study: 76% of grandparents rely on electronic tech as a primary way to stay connected. [AARP 2026](https://datastories.aarp.org/social-leisure/relationships/the-essential-role-of-grandparents/connections-and-relationships/)

**Lesson:** dedicated hardware died, while software on phones people already own survived and was acquired. What remote families do on calls is *sing* and *read*, in sessions of 5–15 minutes, which matches Little Duet's 3–5 minute class length.

## 6. White space for Little Duet (ranked)

1. **True parent–child co-play instruction ("duet") for ages 2–6.** No scaled app puts the parent in the loop as a player.
   - Lingokids, Khan Kids, Sago, CoComelon and Pinkfong are solo play.
   - Music Together and Kindermusik have the parent role but teach no instrument and give no feedback.
   - Prodigies tells parents to clap along but has no duet mechanic.
   - Screenwise notes that co-engagement roughly doubles developmental benefit at ages 2–4. [Screenwise](https://screenwiseapp.com/guides/apps-for-early-childhood-development); [Prodigies guide](https://help.prodigies.com/en/articles/15369740-age-fit-guide-is-prodigies-right-for-my-child-s-age)

2. **"Safe AI" teacher: scripted, on-device listening, no companion, no data leaving the device.**
   - Parents fear data collection (more than 8 in 10) and do not want AI friends (only 19% do).
   - Common Sense says no AI toys under 5.
   - Bondu's data exposure, the Mattel delay and the FTC letter all make open-ended AI chat a liability.
   - A scripted teacher voice plus local pitch detection lets us say "AI that listens, never talks back, never uploads". This also suits the COPPA 2026 rules on retention and third-party consent.
   - [Common Sense](https://www.commonsensemedia.org/research/ai-in-the-toy-box-how-parents-view-ai-enabled-toys-for-young-children); [CapRadio](https://www.capradio.org/articles/2026/02/02/study-warns-ai-toys-pose-unacceptable-risks-to-young-children/); [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/03/ai-toy-bondu-chat-data-exposure-hassan); [NatLawReview](https://natlawreview.com/article/amendments-ftc-coppa-rule-now-effect)

3. **Make the toy piano or xylophone already at home "come alive".**
   - Toy pianos sell for $30–$150.
   - The Melissa & Doug piano relies on a paper color-coded songbook, and Prodigies relies on physical bells.
   - I found no app that listens to a toy instrument and teaches songs on it.
   - Supporting toy-keyboard tuning and limited ranges (25–37 keys) would make us the first to do it, and could support toy-maker bundles.
   - Caveat: no household-ownership data.
   - [Walmart](https://www.walmart.com/browse/toys/pretend-play-dress-up/musical-toys-toy-instruments/4171_4173/Y2F0ZWdvcnk6TXVzaWNhbCBUb3lzICYgVG95IEluc3RydW1lbnRz); [Melissa & Doug](https://melissaanddoug.com/products/learn-to-play-piano)

4. **Grandparent or remote duet.**
   - Grandparents already sing on 5–15 minute video calls.
   - Caribu proved families pay for shared activities on calls (family-shared plan, Mattel exit), and hardware alternatives died.
   - A "play with Grandma" mode, live or asynchronous (a grandparent records their part and the child plays along), has no music competitor I could find.
   - [AARP](https://www.aarp.org/research/topics/technology/info-2021/video-chat-grandparents-grandchildren-pandemic.html); [Caribu](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/caribu/id763451959); [CNBC/Glow](https://www.cnbc.com/2022/10/04/amazon-axes-glow-video-calling-device-for-kids.html)

5. **A $9.99 at-home alternative to $99/mo parent-and-child music classes.**
   - Music Together Online costs $99/mo plus a $149 initial fee. Prodigies costs $19.99/mo.
   - Little Duet's $9.99 sits at the Education median and gives class-style co-music daily, not weekly.
   - The class networks (3,000+ Music Together locations) are also potential partners for practice between classes.
   - [Music Together licensee](https://www.whiletheyrelittle.com/music-together-online.html); [Prodigies](https://prodigies.com/pages/quick-start); [RevenueCat](https://www.revenuecat.com/state-of-subscription-apps-2026-education)

6. **Family plan built for caregivers and siblings from day one.** Peers share across a household or family: Music Together includes siblings, Caribu shares with contacts, Simply offers 5 profiles, and Apple Family Sharing covers up to 6. Grandparents, siblings and nannies should be included in one plan, which strengthens the "duet" story. [RevenueCat Family Sharing](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/apple-family-sharing); [Simply](https://www.hellosimply.com/family/piano-for-kids)

7. **"Screen-light" positioning that rides the screen-free audio boom.**
   - Tonies grew 31% to €630M and Yoto's 2024 revenue rose 86%. Parents pay for less screen.
   - The pitch: the real instrument is the toy and the phone is just the "sheet music".
   - An audio-first mode (teacher voice, eyes on the keys) differentiates from video-heavy Prodigies and screen-centric Lingokids or Pinkfong.
   - [Tonies](https://www.mynewsdesk.com/us/tonies/pressreleases/tonies-continues-profitable-growth-with-record-results-in-2025-expects-strong-momentum-for-full-year-2026-expansion-of-ecosystem-around-toniebox-2-proves-a-global-success-3442746); [RouteNote](https://routenote.com/radar/yoto-revenue-jumps-43-as-screen-free-kids-audio-continues-to-boom/)

8. **"Play the songs they already sing."**
   - Preschool attention is dominated by nursery-rhyme IP: CoComelon's app is set to nursery rhymes, and Pinkfong's YouTube channel has about 280M subscribers.
   - Our 100 public-domain songs overlap with those rhymes. Positioning: "your child already knows the songs; now they can play them with you". Nobody turns that passive listening into playing.
   - [Moonbug](https://www.moonbug.com/news/introducing-cocomelon-kids-learn-play-the-first-learning-app-from-the-cocomelon-universe); [JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/baby-shark-production-company-swims-into-ipo-promises-more-viral-hits/11997863)

9. **Proof-of-progress for parents** (a "parent artifact").
   - Sago built a companion parent app to show what children made, which reinforces the value of paying. [RevenueCat blog](https://www.revenuecat.com/blog/growth/whats-the-best-way-to-monetize-kids-apps.md)
   - Recorded duets, "first song played" clips and a progress map could serve as retention and share hooks.
   - Education trial cohorts keep only about 15–20% of subscribers at month 12, so this matters. [Adapty](https://adapty.io/blog/markdown/education-app-subscription-benchmarks.md)

10. **The ages 2–6 band itself.**
   - The adult and teen piano apps start at about 7 (Simply's testimonials feature ages 7–12, and Prodigies piano starts at 3+).
   - The generic "baby piano" apps are tap toys with ads.
   - The 3–6 "first real lessons" band is effectively unserved at scale. [Simply](https://www.hellosimply.com/family/piano-for-kids); [AppGoblin](https://appgoblin.info/apps/br.com.rodrigokolb.pianokids)
